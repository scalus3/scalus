Whether action stays within the Conway rules the Emulator runs: false for a HardForkInitiation to major 12 or more, spec [EN-4a], D91 (owner, Q8). RATIFY MUST take such an action as not accepted after the checks of [RT-5a] to [RT-5d], so it delays nothing and expires by [RT-10]. The ledger has no such rule: running Conway rules under a PV 12 label would give wrong results silently.
