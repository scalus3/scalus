scalus.cardano.ledger.GovFailure
enum GovFailure

A failure of the Conway GOV rule, one case per constructor of the ledger's ConwayGovPredFailure (eras/conway/impl/src/Cardano/Ledger/Conway/Rules/Gov.hs:180-221), spec [GV-19], D31. The case name is the ledger name. ProposalDepositIncorrect keeps its own exception, and the two PV 9 bootstrap failures are not emulated.
